About

Design, for me, is where engineering and art negotiate. I'm drawn to systematic visual thinking — grids, type hierarchies, geometric abstraction — and equally to the moments where the system breaks down and something unexpected happens.

Most of my graphic work is print-originated: posters, data visualisations, identity work for small projects. I find the constraint of a fixed page more interesting than the infinite canvas of a screen.

The tools change but the question stays the same: what's the clearest possible way to say this?
