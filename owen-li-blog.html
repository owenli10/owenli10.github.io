About

This is where I write when I need to think out loud. Engineering is mostly silent precision — equations, tolerances, simulation runs. Writing is how I process the parts that don't fit into a spreadsheet.

I write about the intersection of technical thinking and the physical world: how things are designed, why they fail, and what it feels like to build something from scratch and have it work — or not.

Posts are infrequent but unhurried. Quality over cadence.
